RoguePod LiteCast

Building the most comprehensive roguelite tier list, one episode at a time. We like playing roguelites, so we started a podcast to talk about them. We spend two weeks playing a game, then review it on the show and rank it against every other game we've covered. Tune in every other Wednesday!

Games covered include Slay the Spire, Hades, Balatro, Noita, Inscryption, The Binding of Isaac, Risk of Rain Returns, Into the Breach, Spelunky 2, Nuclear Throne, Enter the Gungeon, Crypt of the NecroDancer, Rogue Legacy, and many more.
